Carry improves men's historical Brier by .000425, leaving women unchanged. Efficiency improves men's historical Brier by .000270 but worsens women's by .000133; its combined early-period guard still improves .000294. Thus the efficiency gain is not uniformly supported by women's history.

Walk-forward margin-alpha selection chooses .1 for both genders throughout 2015–2026. Within-season GCV chooses men's alpha 1 through 2024, then .1; women's .1 throughout. The existing women's logit already uses .1. Its full-ensemble replacement improves historical Brier only .000081. The alpha grid was [.1,1,10,100]; efficiency tested [1,10,100], carry [1,10], recency [1.5,3], lasso [.01,.1]. These are exploratory sensitivity checks, not an untouched validation set.

Lasso .1 additions fail the early guard in both gender-specific logistic screens (+.001339 M, +.001926 W). Bradley–Terry replacements fail (+.001916 M, +.008458 W). Lasso .01 does not improve historical means; Huber is mixed. Recency/opponent weighting offer small, inconsistent gains. None of the three full candidates fails the early guard, but the two official-metric candidates worsen men's 2021/22 and 2024 versus EXT.

Exact configs are generated by `configs/ratings.py`:

    CARRY = variant(men=['rt_carry10'])
    EFFICIENCY = variant(men=['rt_off1','rt_def1'], women=['rt_off1','rt_def1'])
    WF = variant(women=['rt_wf'], replace=True)

`variant` deep-copies EXT and changes only logit/xgb feature lists, enables `team_ratings=True`, and sets tree `n_jobs=1`. Expanded exact dictionaries are in `*_config.json`. All weights/calibration/blending rules remain inherited. Carry uses prior .7 times previous ordinary rating, penalty 10; efficiency uses offense/defense ridge alpha 1 and home advantage.
